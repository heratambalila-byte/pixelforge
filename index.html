ABOUT PIXELFORGE

Creative work within reach.

PixelForge Digital Solutions is a student-run creative and technology service business. We help people who need strong digital content but may lack the time, tools, or technical skills to make it themselves.

By bringing design, editing, and web development together, we help clients communicate consistently and build a stronger digital presence.

01 / OUR MISSION

Make digital ideas more accessible.

Provide affordable, creative, and reliable digital solutions that help individuals, organizations, and small businesses communicate their ideas and establish a stronger digital presence.

02 / OUR VISION

Become a trusted creative partner.

Be a student-led digital creative service provider known for quality work, accessible pricing, and innovative digital solutions.

HOW PIXELFORGE WORKS

From first conversation to final delivery.

Each stage makes the scope, feedback, and handover clear.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We learn your goals, requirements, audience, preferred design, deadline, and budget.

  2. 02

    Project assessment

    We determine complexity, resources, expected completion time, and pricing.

  3. 03

    Proposal & agreement

    We set out the scope, quotation, timeline, revision limit, and payment terms.

  4. 04

    Content collection

    You provide the text, images, videos, logos, documents, and other needed assets.

  5. 05

    Production

    We begin the design, editing, or development work.

  6. 06

    Client review

    We share a draft or preview for your feedback.

  7. 07

    Revisions

    We make requested changes within the agreed revision limit.

  8. 08

    Final delivery

    We hand over the completed project in the agreed format.
