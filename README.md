8. How PixelForge Works
The entire client process can be simplified into this flow:
Consult → Assess → Quote → Collect → Create → Review → Revise → Deliver
01 — Consultation
Understand the client's goals, requirements, preferred design, audience, deadline, and budget.
02 — Project Assessment
Determine complexity, resources, expected completion time, and pricing.
03 — Proposal & Agreement
Provide the project scope, quotation, timeline, revision limit, and payment terms.
04 — Content Collection
Client provides text, images, videos, logos, documents, and other required assets.
05 — Production
PixelForge begins designing, editing, or developing the project.
06 — Client Review
A draft or preview is presented.
07 — Revisions
Requested changes are made according to the agreed revision limit.
08 — Final Delivery
The completed project is delivered in the agreed format.     PIXELFORGE-DIGITAL-SOLUTIONS
This workflow is especially useful for the portfolio website because it could become a simple "How We Work" section.

14. Important Business Rules
The proposal establishes several important policies.
A 50% initial payment may be required before work starts, with the remaining balance paid before or upon final delivery. Smaller projects may require full upfront payment.
The agreed price only covers the established project scope. Additional requests or revisions can result in additional charges.
Clients are responsible for providing complete project materials. Delays in providing those materials can affect the delivery date.
After final approval, major changes outside the original scope may be considered a new request.
If a project is cancelled after work begins, the initial payment may be retained for work already completed.     PIXELFORGE-DIGITAL-SOLUTIONS
For websites, hosting, domains, premium plugins, paid APIs, and other third-party services are not automatically included unless stated in the quotation. Third-party assets also remain subject to their respective licenses.     PIXELFORGE-DIGITAL-SOLUTIONS
